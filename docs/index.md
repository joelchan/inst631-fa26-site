# INST631 · Fundamentals of Human-Computer Interaction

**Fall 2026 · University of Maryland · Dr. Joel Chan**
Tuesdays, 6:00–8:45pm, HBK 0302H

An introduction to human-computer interaction for HCIM students. You will learn to see the designed world with a design eye, to evaluate interfaces as an expert and with real users, and to design for people who are not interchangeable.

## How the week works

Every week has a **READ**, a **WATCH**, and a **PLAY**. You hand in two things: your play, and a short reflection on all three. Each is worth two points, and I drop your lowest of each, so one lost week costs you nothing.

!!! tip "Start here each week"

    Open that week's quest page, do the three quests, write the reflection. Everything you need for the week is on that one page.


Both are due **midnight the night before class**, because I plan the session from them. The reflection is 250–500 words in three short paragraphs: a **Snag**, a **Compare**, and an **Ask**.

Your play goes on the week's Miro board and your reflection in the week's Google Doc — [here's how that works](where-your-work-goes.md).

Missions are due **by class time**, because we often use them in class. See the [syllabus](syllabus.md) for the late-work policy.
